Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Manookan Fried Chicken House website (the "Site") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the Site has no sign-up or order form. We collect:

  • Information you send us: if you message or email us, we receive your name, contact details and message, and use them only to reply.
  • Usage information: like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may log your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and time of visit.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. The Site itself does not need cookies to work, but the third-party services below may set them.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick (DART) cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are popular. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information in aggregated form. You can prevent it by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to your messages and orders.
  • To understand and improve the Site.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the Site's content free.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Links to other websites

The Site links to services such as Google Maps and Facebook. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

7. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us. Contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
