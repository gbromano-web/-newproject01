About us

About Manookan Fried Chicken House

Manookan Fried Chicken House is a fried chicken stall at 102 Gorordo Ave., Kamputhaw, Cebu City, Philippines. We fry chicken in batches throughout the day and sell every part, wing, drumstick, thigh or breast, at one fair price of ₱25.

What this website is for

This website has two jobs. First, it tells you exactly what we sell: our menu, prices, ingredients, allergens and how we cook. Second, our blog shares practical food guides drawn from frying chicken every day, such as how to reheat leftovers so they stay crispy and how to store them safely in Cebu's heat.

Who writes our content

Articles are written and reviewed by the Manookan team, the same people who run the fryer. Manookan is run by Nixon Romano, who has been frying chicken on Gorordo Avenue since 2024.

Food safety tips follow widely published guidance on storing and reheating cooked food. We don't give medical or nutrition advice. If you have an allergy or a health condition, ask us directly and check with your doctor.

How we write our guides

  • From the fryer. Tips about coating, frying, holding and reheating come from what we do every day at the stall, adapted for home kitchens.
  • Checked against official sources. Food-safety numbers, like the 74 °C (165 °F) safe internal temperature for chicken and the 2-hour rule for leftovers, follow published guidance from food-safety agencies such as the US Department of Agriculture (USDA).
  • Dated and updated. Every article shows when it was last updated. When we learn something new or find a mistake, we fix the article and change the date.
  • Honest about what we don't know. If we haven't measured something yet, such as exact calories per piece, we say so instead of guessing.

Our promise

  • Honest prices: the price on our menu is the price you pay.
  • Honest information: if we don't know something, or haven't measured it yet, we say so.
  • Corrections: if you spot a mistake on this site, tell us and we'll fix it.
