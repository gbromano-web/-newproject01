About Manookan Fried Chicken House

Manookan Fried Chicken House is a fried chicken stall at 102 Gorordo Ave., Kamputhaw, Cebu City, Philippines. We fry chicken in batches throughout the day and sell every part, wing, drumstick, thigh or breast, at one fair price of ₱25.

What this website is for

This website has two jobs. First, it tells you exactly what we sell: our menu, prices, ingredients, allergens and how we cook. Second, our blog shares practical food guides drawn from frying chicken every day, such as how to reheat leftovers so they stay crispy and how to store them safely in Cebu's heat.
